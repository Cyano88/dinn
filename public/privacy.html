Privacy Policy

This draft describes the current application design. The final policy will identify the operator, contact details, service providers and retention practices before sign-in opens.

Account information

When configured, Dinn uses Privy for Google or X sign-in and embedded wallet access. The application uses the account information returned by Privy to display your profile and associate your workspace with your account.

Browser storage

Current Pons briefs and AI conversations use local browser storage, separated by account. Earlier Elysium drafts and recovery records remain in their original session or local storage.

Requests and connected services

Planning requests reach the Dinn server. When inference is connected, messages are sent to the configured AI service. Read-only network and balance checks send relevant blockchain addresses to the configured RPC provider.

Public blockchain records

Transactions submitted to a public blockchain may remain visible independently of Dinn. Clearing browser storage does not remove blockchain records.

Before publication

The final policy will explain retention, deletion requests, provider processing, any analytics, international transfers and applicable privacy rights.
