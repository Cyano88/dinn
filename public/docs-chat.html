Using Dinn Chat

Research a token, launch one on Pons, or manage your wallet in one conversation. Sign in with X or Google to begin.

The default allowance is 50 completed chat replies per signed-in account per day, resetting at midnight UTC. Guided steps handled in the browser, wallet approvals, cancellations and receipt checks use no replies. Retrying an interrupted request in the same chat uses at most one reply.

Start with a question

Why did Tesla move today?

A company name works. For supported U.S. stocks, Dinn checks the price against the previous close and looks for dated news. Open the source to see what happened. An issuer announcement can confirm an event; its timing alone cannot explain a price move.

Research NVDAc on Base. Who holds the most?

For a stock token, give the company name or ticker and network. For a token launched on Bankr or Pons, paste the launch token's contract address and name the chain. Ask a follow-up like Is that a pool? Dinn can identify a known pool when its address matches. A list of sampled holders may omit larger addresses.

Read a report

  • Price. Check the timestamp and whether the figure is a U.S. share price, issuer reference value, or onchain pool price. Ask Did the token follow it? For a supported Base stock token, Dinn compares the stock move with trades in one Base pool near the previous U.S. close and the quoted time. If either price is missing, the token return stays unknown.
  • Trading. Pool liquidity and recent volume describe activity. Ask for a fresh quote to see what a particular trade could receive.
  • Locks and vesting. Ask how many tokens are locked and when they unlock. Open the locker record where available. An empty search leaves the lock status unknown.

Launch on Pons

Say Launch a token on Pons. Dinn guides you through the token details, available supply presets, trading pair and fees. You can add a logo, links and a website, preview a Dinn-generated page, and choose an optional opening purchase. Review the launch wallet and costs before approving. Use the transaction link to check the result.

You can leave setup to research a token or check your balance. Say Back to my launch to resume your saved progress. Bankr token creation through Dinn is coming soon.

Use your wallet

Try Check my ETH balance on Base or Send ETH on Base. For a transfer, Dinn needs the network, recipient and amount. For a swap or bridge, it also needs the asset you want to receive. Check those details and the fee in the review, then approve or cancel. A bridge has separate source and destination confirmations.

Find a launch or use a tool

Open Launches to find a token and start its research flow. In Dinn tools, pick the network to see available actions. For a Pons launch, ask about the bonding curve, creator fees, or a token lock. Creating a lock takes you to the external locker. Claiming eligible fees requires a separate wallet review.
